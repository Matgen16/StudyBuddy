Your recordings and transcripts are not shared with any third-party services, cloud providers, or external parties. All processing happens on the administrator's private server. No data is sold or disclosed to outside organisations.

You should be aware that recordings stored on the server may be accessible to the server administrator. Do not record sensitive personal conversations or confidential information.
